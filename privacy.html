Privacy Policy – Trung Bia Scheduler

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Trung Bia Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owners of "Chuyện Bịa Mỗi Ngày" and "Trứng Chưa Chín": two Facebook Pages and their own accounts on TikTok and other platforms. It schedules and publishes our own posts and videos on those accounts and replies to comments left on our posts.

What data the app uses

  • Content of our own Pages and accounts: posts and videos we create, and the comments people leave on them (to reply and to count engagement).
  • Public profile name of people who comment on our posts, only as shown in the comment, to address the reply.
  • TikTok: when we connect our own TikTok account through "Login with TikTok", the app receives that account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and an access token, so it can upload and publish our own videos to it. No other TikTok user logs in to the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not offer login to the public and does not collect personal data from visitors.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not message anyone who has not messaged our Page first.
  • It does not read other TikTok users' videos, followers or messages.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only in the owner's computer keychain. Comment data is read when needed and is not kept in a database. Revoking the app's access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or Facebook deletes its access at once.

Deleting your data

To have a comment or reply removed, or to ask any question about this policy, send a message to either Page on Facebook:

  • https://www.facebook.com/chuyenbiamoingay
  • https://www.facebook.com/trungchuachin.vn

We remove the requested content within 7 days.
